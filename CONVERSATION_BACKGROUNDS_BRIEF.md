# Conversation Background Brief

30 background scenes for `ConversationBox`, in the style of the first delivered piece
(`assets/backgrounds/harbour_tavern_dusk_1.png` — Tortuga Cove harbor at dusk). Every scene below
is a real location that already exists in the game data (`src/data/islands.ts`, `buildings.ts`,
`pirateLords.ts`, `landmarks.ts`), not invented dressing, so each image maps onto an actual place
the player visits.

## Paste this once, reuse for every image

> Painterly pixel-art background illustration for a mobile pirate RPG, in the vein of modern
> "HD-2D" JRPG scene art — richly detailed and atmospheric, not blocky/retro 8-bit. Rich, warm,
> slightly desaturated color grading; visible but fine pixel-art texture, not photorealistic.
> Caribbean pirate-era setting: real wood, stone, rope, canvas sails, tropical foliage — no magic,
> no fantasy creatures, no modern elements.
>
> **Orientation**: tall vertical portrait, notably taller than a normal phone screen — target
> canvas is 853×1844px (~9:19.5). If your tool only offers fixed sizes, use the tallest portrait
> option available (e.g. DALL·E 3's 1024×1792) and I'll crop/fit it.
>
> **Composition rule (important — this is a UI background, not standalone art)**: the image sits
> behind a dialogue box and character portrait that are overlaid on the *bottom third* of the
> screen in-game. Keep roughly the bottom 35–40% of the canvas visually simple and low-detail —
> open ground, plain path, calm water, or gently out-of-focus — so text and a character sprite
> stay readable on top of it. Put the rich detail (buildings, ships, skyline, foliage, props) in
> the upper 60–65%.
>
> **No people, no creatures, no text, no UI** baked into the image — characters are composited on
> top separately. One continuous scene, not a collage or vignette grid.

## The 30 scenes

### Tortuga Cove — home port, safe zone (4)

1. **`tortuga_market_day_1`** — Tortuga Cove's harbor square at midday, market stalls open, fishing
   boats at the pier, sun-bleached stone buildings with red tile roofs, a busy but calm hometown
   feel — contrast with the existing dusk piece by making this one bright and bustling.
2. **`tortuga_tavern_night_1`** — The Salty Parrot tavern exterior at night: warm lantern light
   spilling from windows and an open door, a hand-painted parrot sign, cobblestones underfoot,
   quiet street, a couple of barrels and crates stacked by the wall.
3. **`tortuga_gaol_interior_1`** — Interior of The Gaol: a small stone jail cell, iron bars, a
   single barred window letting in weak daylight, damp stone floor, a low wooden bench — tense,
   spare, cramped.
4. **`tortuga_old_landing_dusk_1`** — The Ruins of the Old Landing at the edge of Tortuga Cove:
   a collapsed, overgrown dock structure being reclaimed by jungle, broken pilings in shallow
   water, late-afternoon light, a faded sense of history.

### Cow Island — Redbeard Sully's territory (3)

5. **`cow_island_beach_camp_1`** — Cow Island Beach Camp: low grazing flats meeting a scrubby
   beach, a driftwood-and-canvas pirate camp, a cook-fire, grey overcast morning light, real cattle
   grazing distantly.
6. **`cow_island_muster_flats_1`** — The open grazing flats themselves, wide and flat, where real
   pirate fleets once mustered before a raid — tall grass, a few scattered ship masts visible on
   the horizon, dramatic late-day sky.
7. **`lord_redbeard_sully_fort_1`** — Redbeard Sully's stronghold: a rough, fortified timber-and-
   stone camp on Cow Island, cutlasses and a red pirate flag, bonfire smoke, gritty and lawless —
   he's "the Reaver of Cow Island," first Pirate Lord, so this should feel entry-level tough, not
   yet grand.

### New Providence — the pirate republic (3)

8. **`new_providence_harbor_1`** — Harbor Trading Post, New Providence: a chaotic, crowded pirate-
   republic waterfront at midday — no flags of any nation, mismatched ships of every design docked
   together, hand-painted trade signs, sun-bleached wood.
9. **`new_providence_tavern_1`** — The Cracked Hull tavern's porch at night: raucous energy implied
   through warm spilled lantern light and a cracked, patched-together timber facade, more chaotic
   and lawless-feeling than Tortuga's tavern.
10. **`lord_iron_jenny_fort_1`** — Iron Jenny's stronghold, "Queen of the Republic": a fortified
    manor or compound with sharpshooter nests, muskets racked along a wall, an eagle motif, proud
    and militant rather than scrappy — she's a musket specialist and duelist.

### Roatán — the careening cove (3)

11. **`roatan_careening_yard_1`** — The Careening Yard: ship hulls tilted on their sides on the
    beach for repair and re-arming, scaffolding, tar barrels, tools scattered, working shipyard
    energy, late-afternoon light.
12. **`roatan_forge_night_1`** — The Anchor & Forge blacksmith at dusk/night: an open-air forge
    glowing orange, anchors and cannon parts mid-repair, sparks, smoke curling up.
13. **`lord_captain_bellows_fort_1`** — Captain Bellows' cove stronghold, "Lord of Roatán": cannon
    emplacements along a rocky shoreline, gun carriages, powder kegs — he's a cannon specialist
    commanding "the careening yards... with an iron gun crew," so make artillery the visual focus.

### Port Royal — the sunken city (3)

14. **`port_royal_flooded_street_1`** — A half-submerged colonial street in Port Royal: buildings
    tilted and partially swallowed by the sea after the earthquake, brackish water reflecting an
    overcast blue-grey dusk sky, an eerie, drowned-city mood.
15. **`port_royal_customs_house_1`** — The Customs House: crumbling colonial stone architecture,
    cracked columns, overgrown with salt-tolerant vines, government grandeur gone to ruin.
16. **`lord_marietta_graves_lair_1`** — Marietta Graves' lair, "the Drowned Widow": a flooded,
    ghostly underwater-adjacent ruin — sunken chandeliers, waterlogged furniture, a pale
    blue-green cursed light, since her specialty is "curse" and she "drowned once... and came back
    captain of the ruins."

### Île Sainte-Marie — the remote legendary haven (3)

17. **`ile_sainte_marie_square_1`** — Republic Square at foggy dawn: a remote, semi-mythical pirate
    utopia settlement, low stone buildings emerging from mist, quiet and isolated, a sense of being
    at the edge of the known map.
18. **`ile_sainte_marie_marked_palm_1`** — The Marked Palm landmark: a single tall palm tree with a
    skull carved into its trunk, standing alone against a moody sunset sky — an ominous waypoint.
19. **`lord_ezra_vane_fortress_1`** — Ezra Vane's fortress, "the Last Free Captain": built around or
    beside a genuine whirlpool in the sea, a strange half-natural half-fortified structure, swirling
    water motif, since his epithet/lair is literally "my whirlpool."

### Ocracoke Inlet — the endgame, Blackbeard's waters (3)

20. **`ocracoke_storm_shallows_1`** — The treacherous shallow waters of Ocracoke Inlet under
    gathering storm clouds: sandbars just breaking the surface, choppy grey-green water, a sense of
    real danger — "the endgame of these seas."
21. **`lord_blackbeard_camp_1`** — Blackbeard's fort/camp at Ocracoke: the most imposing, battle-worn
    stronghold in the game — cannons, a huge black flag, driftwood fortifications, dramatic
    stormlight, since he's the final, hardest Pirate Lord.
22. **`ocracoke_final_stand_1`** — The shoreline site of "Blackbeard's last stand": scattered
    wreckage half-buried in sand, a broken mast, dramatic storm-breaking-to-gold light — this is
    the game's climactic dialogue backdrop, so give it real weight.

### The Black Pearl & open sea (3)

23. **`black_pearl_deck_day_1`** — The Black Pearl's main deck in daylight: coiled rope, the
    mainmast and rigging rising into a bright sky, wind-filled black sails, ocean visible over the
    rail — the ship the player actually captures and sails.
24. **`black_pearl_captains_quarters_1`** — Interior of the Black Pearl's captain's quarters at
    night: a map table lit by a single lantern, charts, a compass, gun-rack, warm low light through
    a stern window looking out on dark water.
25. **`open_sea_helm_dusk_1`** — Standing at the helm mid-voyage, open ocean in every direction,
    dusk sky, the ship's wheel and bow visible in the foreground-but-still-simple lower band, no
    land in sight.

### Landmarks & wilderness (5)

26. **`lighthouse_night_1`** — The Lighthouse (Tortuga landmark) at night: its beam sweeping out
    over dark water, rocky coastline, a single dramatic light source.
27. **`shipwreck_santa_catalina_1`** — Wreck of the Santa Catalina: a broken hull half-buried in a
    shallow reef, timbers jutting from turquoise water, sun-bleached and eerie, midday.
28. **`smugglers_grotto_1`** — The Smugglers' Grotto: a hidden sea cave interior, contraband crates
    stacked on wet rock, a shaft of light from a hole above, water lapping at the cave mouth.
29. **`high_woods_jungle_1`** — The High Woods: a dense, overgrown jungle path, thick canopy letting
    through dappled green-gold light, roots and undergrowth — matches the game's existing "jungle"
    encounter-backdrop category.
30. **`generic_pier_night_1`** — A generic, reusable dockside/pier scene at night: simple wooden
    pier, a couple of moored rowboats, lanterns, calm dark water — a flexible fallback for any
    minor waterfront conversation that isn't tied to a specific named location.
